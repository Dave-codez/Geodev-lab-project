# My Project brief

## The question
Flood exposure to buildings, roads, and population in Okpanam, Asaba, Delta state.

## The data i need 
- Facilities and settlement---GRID3 Data Hub - data.grid3.org
- Topography--- OpenTopography - portal.opentopography.org
- Population---WorldPop - worldpop.org
- Rainfall estimate---CHIRPS - chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps
- Climate and weather condition---NiMet, the Nigerian Meteorological Agency - nimet.gov.ng
- Human ststistics National Bureau of Statistics - nigerianstat.gov.ng
- Extract waterways---HOT exports on HDX - data.humdata.org

## What I want to build 
I want to build a system that shows the area with un-navigable road network after rainfall and shows how the water gathers to the particular point.



## Why it matters
It gives descriptions to how prone certain areas is to flood and gives insight on how to manage and combat flooding in these areas


## Data used
Building data, road network data, elevation/soil data, population
